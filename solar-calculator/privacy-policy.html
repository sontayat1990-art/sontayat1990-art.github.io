Privacy Policy – Solar Sizing Calculator

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the "Solar Sizing Calculator" Android/iOS app ("the App") handles information. The App is published by an individual developer (contact: sontaya.t1990@gmail.com).

Data you enter

Projects, appliance lists, system settings and app settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the App deletes them. PDF and CSV files are created on your device and shared only with the apps you choose.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and usage/diagnostic data to deliver, measure and personalise ads. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change their choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play / App Store via RevenueCat)

Payments are processed by Google Play or Apple. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts to verify the Pro unlock. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at this address.
